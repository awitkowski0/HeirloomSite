Description

Solid hardwood 4-in-1 convertible crib made in the USA. The gentle curve of the Arched Top adds soft elegance to any nursery, and it is crafted from real solid hardwood - no particle board, no MDF, no veneer - so the piece has the weight, durability, and longevity parents expect from furniture that will serve from newborn through the teen years.It converts cleanly through four stages: full-size crib to toddler bed, daybed, and full-size bed. Conversion kits are included where stated on this product page, so one investment grows with your child. Three adjustable mattress positions let you lower the mattress as your baby becomes more mobile while keeping a firm, flat sleep surface that meets applicable federal standards.Finished with non-toxic, water-based, lead-free materials. Meets ASTM F1169 full-size crib standard, JPMA specifications, and CPSIA requirements.
