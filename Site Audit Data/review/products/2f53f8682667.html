Description

The ultimate nursery storage solution, the French Country 3 Drawer Dresser with Door and Changing Table from Old Time Oak combines everything you need in one stunning piece. Three drawers, a cabinet, and a dedicated changing station come together in a beautifully crafted solid wood dresser. The spacious changing table top provides a safe, comfortable surface for diaper changes, with a raised back to prevent accidents. Three dovetailed drawers offer organized storage for daily essentials, while the enclosed cabinet with door is perfect for larger items, diaper boxes, or items you want tucked away. Each drawer glides smoothly on full-extension hardware for easy access. Crafted from solid wood with the signature French Country charm, this piece is built to last through multiple children and beyond. When the diaper years end, the changing table can be removed to reveal a versatile three-drawer dresser with cabinet that works beautifully in any bedroom. Non-toxic, baby-safe finish.
