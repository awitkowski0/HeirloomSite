Description

Solid hardwood 4-in-1 convertible crib made in the USA. The Newport is crafted from real solid hardwood with a warm Brown Maple finish - no particle board, no MDF, no veneer - so the piece has the weight, durability, and longevity parents expect from furniture that will serve from newborn through the teen years.It converts cleanly through four stages: full-size crib to toddler bed, daybed, and full-size bed. Conversion kits are included where stated on this product page, so one investment grows with your child. Three adjustable mattress positions let you lower the mattress as your baby becomes more mobile while keeping a firm, flat sleep surface that meets applicable federal standards.Finished with non-toxic, water-based, lead-free materials. Select models carry GREENGUARD Gold certification for low chemical emissions. Every crib meets or exceeds ASTM F1169 (full-size crib standard), CPSC 16 CFR 1219 requirements, and JPMA specifications.
