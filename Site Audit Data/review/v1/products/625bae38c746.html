Description

Stock your nursery with confidence using the French Country 6 Drawer Dresser from Old Time Oak. With six spacious dovetailed drawers, this dresser provides ample room for your growing child's wardrobe, from baby onesies through school-age favorites. Each drawer features full-extension glides for easy access to items at the back, and the sturdy dovetail construction ensures lasting durability.
