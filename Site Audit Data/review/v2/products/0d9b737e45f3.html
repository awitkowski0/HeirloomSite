Description

Extend the life of your French Country crib with the Full Bed Rail Kit from Old Time Oak. This complete conversion set transforms your crib into a full-size bed frame, giving your growing child a familiar and comfortable sleep space for years to come. The kit includes everything you need: side rails, hardware, and instructions for a straightforward installation. Crafted from solid wood with a beautiful finish that matches your French Country furniture, the rails integrate seamlessly with the existing headboard and footboard from your crib conversion. Moving from toddler bed to full-size bed is an exciting milestone, and this kit makes it feel like a natural progression. Your child gets to keep the furniture they love while gaining the space and maturity of a big bed. All materials are non-toxic and baby-safe. Compatible with OTO French Country crib models.
