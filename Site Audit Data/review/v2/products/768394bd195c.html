Description

When nothing but the best will do, choose the French Country 7 Drawer Dresser with Changing Table from Old Time Oak. This magnificent piece offers seven dovetailed drawers of storage plus a dedicated changing station, making it the ultimate nursery investment. After the diaper years, simply remove the changing table to reveal a stunning seven-drawer dresser that transitions beautifully into a child's or teen's bedroom.
